Review

I ranked Persona 4 Golden 4th because it’s a charming and fun game, even if it’s not my absolute favorite. The style is bright and colorful with a cozy small-town feel, and the characters are lovable and full of personality. The story is engaging, mixing mystery and humor, though it doesn’t hit the same emotional depth as the other persona games. Gameplay is solid, with turn-based combat and daily life simulation that keeps things interesting, and the music is upbeat and catchy, perfectly matching the game’s tone. Overall, it’s a heartwarming and enjoyable experience that’s easy to get invested in.
