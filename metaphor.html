Review

I ranked Metaphor: ReFantazio 3rd because it’s a really creative and visually striking game. The style is colorful and full of personality, with unique areas and memorable character designs. The story is engaging, though it doesn’t have the emotional impact of Persona 3 or Persona 5, and some characters could use more development. Gameplay is solid and rewarding, with fun combat and exploration that keeps you interested, while the music perfectly complements the world, making battles and exploration feel lively and exciting. Overall, it’s a charming and enjoyable RPG that’s definitely worth experiencing.
