Review

I ranked Persona 5 Royal 2nd because it’s stylish, fun, and impossible not to get attached to the cast. The style is slick and full of personality, with incredible visuals and a vibrant city to explore. The story is engaging and full of twists, and I became really invested in the characters. Their growth and interactions kept me hooked the whole way through. Gameplay is smooth and rewarding, combining turn-based combat with life-sim elements, and the music is unforgettable, perfectly capturing the game’s rebellious and energetic tone. Overall, it’s a standout RPG that’s hard not to love.
